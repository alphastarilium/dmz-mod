# dmz-mod

QoL/light balancing mod for EU4 based from LIVID. Credit to the original creator of livid since like 85% of this is from it. This was originally an updated version of livid maintained by me for more recent versions of the game with the necessary fixes for said EU4 updates.

# changes/fixes made by me listed below
full livid changes can be seen in the changelog.txt

Arsonists Version Changelog (1.37.2+)

Fixes
- Restored free cities
- Fixed the bug of several missing reform tiers for republics, meaning they could not progress beyond tier 1 due to there being no reforms for tiers 2 or 3
- Austro-Hungarian and Great Moravian ideas restored (Forming the tags and taking new ideas would previously just give the country generic ideas)
- Missing burgher estate privileges restored
- Fixed Austria-Hungary not being able to take the Austrian age bonus in the Age of Revolutions
- Fixed crashes caused by the Khmer 'Second Golden Age' and 'Khmer Resurgence' missions


Balancing/New Features
- Swedish Recruitment age bonus (previously only available to Scandinavia and Sweden) now also made available to Denmark
- Provinces of Interest added to Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Asia
- Khmer 'Second Golden Age' mission nerfed from -25% all powers cost to -5%
- German regional formable ideas updated
- Ruthenian, Moroccan, Breton, and Burgundian ideas updated
- Colonist added to the ideas of Brunei and Madyas
- Prussian ideas nerfed (Morale & ICA) & militarization reworked
- Ideas of Persia & Ottomans reworked
- Zoroastrian Shirvan monument nerfed 
- Scaling trade efficiency debuff applied/scaled with each age
- Quantity ideas forcelimit nerfed 33% to 25%


Other Changes 
- China Republic renamed & Zhongguo's main color changed from { 222 111 161 } to { 224 48 48 }
- China gov options now all have unique colors
